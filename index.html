Where the numbers come from

Places are geocoded with Nominatim and roads routed with OSRM, both OpenStreetMap projects — so any town works and nothing is hardcoded. Fuel is your car's L/100 km × your $/litre × the real routed distance. Lodging is your nightly rates × nights. Everything else is a line you enter.
Not looked up: fuel prices, hotel rates and ferry fares — this page asks you for them rather than inventing them. Unpriced stops are counted, not guessed.
