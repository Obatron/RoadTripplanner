Where the numbers come from

Places are geocoded with Nominatim and roads routed with OSRM, both OpenStreetMap projects — so any town works and nothing is hardcoded. The map draws the real road geometry it returns. If a lookup fails you get a box to type the distance rather than a made-up number.
Crossing time, check-in window, nightly rates and fuel are yours to set. Not researched: hotel rates and fuel price.
